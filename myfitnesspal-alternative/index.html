A MyFitnessPal alternative that also tracks your gym sessions.

MyFitnessPal is the best-known calorie counter there is. Nutrio+ is a newer iPhone app that puts calorie counting and workout logging in one place. Here's how they compare, and when MyFitnessPal is still the better pick. Prices and features checked on 21 October 2026.

When MyFitnessPal is the better choice

  • You're on Android. Nutrio+ is iPhone only for now.
  • You only want to count calories and you want to do it for free. MyFitnessPal's free version logs food (you type or search it in, with ads). Nutrio+'s free version is for training; food logging needs Premium.
  • You pay yearly and price is all that matters. MyFitnessPal Premium is £64.99 a year against £79.99 for Nutrio+.
  • You want the biggest food database there is. MyFitnessPal has been building its database since 2005.

When Nutrio+ is the better choice

  • You go to the gym and you're tired of running one app for food and another for workouts. Nutrio+ does both, and your meals and sessions sit on one home screen.
  • You'd rather pay monthly. £8.99 a month against £15.99.
  • You don't want ads anywhere, including the free version.
  • You shop in UK supermarkets and want allergen flags that match UK labelling.
  • You keep going when there's something to lose. Nutrio+ has a streak for food and one for training, XP, levels and leagues with friends, ranked on XP only, never on weight.

Questions

Switching from MyFitnessPal.

Is Nutrio+ cheaper than MyFitnessPal?

Monthly, yes: £8.99 against £15.99 for MyFitnessPal Premium. Yearly, no: £79.99 against £64.99. Prices checked on 21 October 2026.

Can I log food for free on Nutrio+?

No. The free Starter plan covers workout logging, streaks, achievements and leagues. Food logging is part of Premium, which has a 3-day free trial.

Does Nutrio+ have a barcode scanner?

Yes, in Premium. It's built for UK supermarket products, and it flags the 14 UK FSA allergens.

Can I import my MyFitnessPal history?

Not at the moment. You start fresh, and Nutrio+ works out new targets from your body and your goal when you sign up.

Is Nutrio+ on Android?

Not yet. It's iPhone only for now, iOS 16 or later.
