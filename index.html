Questions

Before you
download.

Is there a free version?

A 3-day free trial on every plan. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you're not charged.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID subscription settings, like any App Store subscription. Apple handles billing — we never see your card.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. iPhone only, and built natively — Apple Health, home-screen and lock-screen widgets all work properly.

How accurate is the AI?

Strong on recognisable meals and packaged food, less certain on mixed dishes. Edit anything it gets wrong — it learns how you portion.

Can I trust it with allergies?

As an extra check, never your only one. It screens all 14 UK FSA allergens.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes — it reads your steps and calories burned, and writes workouts back.

Is there a widget?

Three home-screen sizes plus a lock-screen one, showing calories, macros, water and both streaks.
