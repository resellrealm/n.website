How it works

Snap it. Check it. Done.

  1. Take a photo of your plate. It picks out what's on it, even mixed dishes like a poke bowl.
  2. Check the numbers. Calories, protein, carbs and fat, plus fibre, sugar and salt. Wrong portion? Change it and it learns how you eat.
  3. Tap Add. It goes into your day and your rings fill up.

Not in the mood for a photo? Four other ways.

  • Scan the barcodeBuilt for UK supermarket shelves.
  • Type it"Two eggs on toast and a flat white."
  • Say itHands full? Just talk to it.
  • Snap a recipeA cookbook page becomes a logged meal.

Questions

Before you download.

Is there a free version?

There's a 3-day free trial on every plan. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID subscription settings, like any App Store subscription. Apple handles billing, so we never see your card.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. It's iPhone only for now, built natively so Apple Health and the home-screen and lock-screen widgets all work properly.

How accurate is the photo logging?

Very good on recognisable meals and packaged food, less sure on mixed dishes. You can edit anything it gets wrong, and it learns how you portion.

Can I trust it with allergies?

Use it as an extra check, never your only one. It screens all 14 UK FSA allergens, but AI can miss things, so with a serious allergy always read the packaging.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. It reads your steps and calories burned, and writes your workouts back.

Is there a widget?

Three home-screen sizes plus a lock-screen one, showing calories, macros, water and both streaks.
